# Bottlenecks

## Candidate bottlenecks in the AI-to-reality chain

- trustworthy context and provenance
- identity and counterparty resolution
- authority and institutional permission
- economic meaning attached to events
- contradiction detection and resolution
- physical-world observation and execution
- evidence of execution and resulting state
- accountability, dispute resolution, and learning from outcomes

## Current wedge bottlenecks to test

1. **Fragmentation:** evidence exists across financial and operational systems.
2. **Semantic ambiguity:** transactions record movement, not necessarily economic meaning.
3. **Verification:** important claims may require invoices, inventory, contracts, delivery, or counterparty confirmation.
4. **Contradictions:** claims and sources may conflict.
5. **Decision fitness:** an output must meet a decision-maker's standard, not merely look coherent.
6. **Incumbent reproducibility:** existing institutions may already possess enough data, distribution, and authority to build the capability internally.

## Unknown

Which bottleneck is both structurally important and independently ownable has not been established.
