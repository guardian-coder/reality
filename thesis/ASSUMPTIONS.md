# Assumptions Register

Every item below is a hypothesis until evidence changes its status.

| ID | Assumption | Current status | How to test |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-01 | AI will continue making cognition, prediction, planning, and software action cheaper. | Plausible; not proven here | Track capability, cost, and adoption evidence |
| A-02 | Scarcity will migrate toward context, authority, permission, physical capacity, verified outcomes, and accountability. | Core hypothesis | Compare durable bottlenecks and willingness to pay across domains |
| A-03 | Existing SME digital traces do not contain enough economic meaning for decision-grade reconstruction. | Critical unknown | Run trace-reconstruction tests and expert review |
| A-04 | Missing meaning can be recovered through a new evidence/verification layer. | Unknown | Identify required evidence and test acquisition/reconciliation |
| A-05 | Better economic truth changes a consequential decision. | Unknown | Test with lenders, suppliers, insurers, investors, and SMEs |
| A-06 | At least one actor will pay for this improvement. | Unknown | Pricing and commitment tests with candidate payers |
| A-07 | The capability can be independently defensible rather than copied by incumbents. | Unknown | Build-versus-buy interviews and competitive analysis |
| A-08 | Evidence → decision → outcome loops can compound into a proprietary asset. | Speculative | Define feedback data and test access/retention rights |
| A-09 | SME finance is a strong first wedge into the wider reality problem. | Selected for testing | Compare with alternative wedges on structural importance and falsifiability |
| A-10 | Trusted execution/delegated authority may be a deeper primitive than truth. | Unresolved | Architecture comparison using concrete workflows |

## Assumptions explicitly rejected as planning rules

- “A solo founder cannot build it now” is not evidence that a thesis is unimportant.
- “The founder is in Tanzania” is not evidence that Tanzania must define the final market.
- “Access to finance is a large problem” does not prove that lack of financial truth is its cause.
