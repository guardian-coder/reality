# Kill Criteria

These criteria are provisional and must be made quantitative as validation produces evidence.

## Kill or substantially revise the SME financial-truth wedge if

1. Existing bank, mobile-money, accounting, tax, and related digital traces plus current AI reconstruct the relevant economic state with decision-maker-acceptable accuracy, provenance, cost, and latency—without a new evidence layer.
2. Missing meaning exists but cannot be acquired or verified legally, economically, or operationally.
3. Improved truth does not materially change a consequential decision such as credit, trade credit, insurance, investment, procurement, or business management.
4. No plausible payer makes a credible commitment to adopt or pay after seeing a concrete evidence-backed output.
5. Banks, credit bureaus, accounting platforms, or mobile-money operators can reproduce the full value with structurally superior data and distribution, leaving no independently defensible layer.
6. The output cannot expose provenance, uncertainty, and contradictions well enough to meet the relevant decision standard.
7. The wedge requires becoming a lender or assuming risk that is not intrinsic to the infrastructure thesis.

Transaction-based risk prediction alone is no longer sufficient evidence for the wedge. Existing practice already demonstrates that capability. Survival now requires measurable incremental decision value from verified economic context beyond the lender's current scorecard and diligence process.

## Kill or substantially revise the candidate invariant if

1. Better models repeatedly make external verification unnecessary for consequential decisions.
2. The dominant bottleneck in concrete workflows is authority, regulation, physical capacity, or distribution rather than establishing real-world state.
3. Actors accept probabilistic prediction and will not pay or change behavior for greater defensibility.
4. Incumbents already deliver sufficient verification cheaply using data and institutional access they control.
5. Evidence-to-state methods remain too bespoke to standardize, reproduce, or improve across cases.
6. The cost of verification consistently exceeds the expected cost of decision error.
7. Apparent cross-domain recurrence reduces to superficial analogy rather than the same structural constraint.

## Revise the umbrella thesis if

- AI-to-reality reliability does not emerge as a meaningful bottleneck in concrete, consequential workflows; or
- the proposed scarce layers are consistently features captured by existing systems rather than independently valuable infrastructure.

## Evidence standard

A kill decision must cite observed evidence and the tested workflow. Absence of convenient access, solo-founder feasibility, or current capital is not by itself a kill criterion.
