# Validation Plan

## Objective

Falsify the working wedge before substantial product development.

## Primary question

Can existing digital traces plus modern AI already reconstruct SME economic truth well enough for a specific consequential decision?

## Phase 1 — Define one decision and truth standard

Choose one concrete SME archetype and one decision (for example lending, trade credit, insurance, investment, procurement, or internal management). Selection is not yet made.

Document:

- the decision-maker;
- facts required;
- acceptable confidence, provenance, latency, and cost;
- consequences of false positives and false negatives;
- current process and baseline outcome.

## Phase 2 — Trace reconstruction benchmark

Using consented, lawful, representative data:

1. inventory all available digital traces;
2. classify facts as observable, inferable, unverifiable, or requiring external evidence;
3. run a current AI-only reconstruction baseline;
4. have domain experts assess correctness and decision fitness;
5. add candidate evidence sources and measure improvement;
6. record contradictions, unresolved ambiguity, cost, and latency.

## Phase 3 — Demand and decision-change tests

Interview candidate users and payers without assuming they are the same actor:

- SME owner
- lender
- supplier offering trade credit
- insurer
- investor
- procurement or audit actor

Test whether the output changes a real or shadow decision. Seek behavioral evidence: data access, pilot participation, a letter of intent, paid test, or another concrete commitment. Compliments and general interest are weak evidence.

## Phase 4 — Incumbent and defensibility test

Assess whether banks, credit bureaus, accounting platforms, mobile-money operators, and data aggregators can reproduce the result. Identify what, if anything, remains uniquely acquirable, verifiable, or compounding.

## Phase 5 — Architecture comparison

Map the same workflow twice:

- truth-first: evidence → decision-grade state → decision/action;
- authority-first: principal → mandate → constraints → action → execution evidence.

Determine which layer contains the unresolved bottleneck and whether both are necessary.

## Decision outputs

End validation with one explicit outcome:

- proceed with the wedge;
- revise the wedge;
- elevate trusted execution/delegated authority;
- define a combined architecture; or
- kill the thesis under documented criteria.

## Three highest-leverage next actions

1. Choose one SME archetype and one consequential decision for the benchmark.
2. Run the trace-reconstruction falsification with real, consented sample evidence and an expert-defined standard.
3. Resolve the truth-versus-authority hierarchy using that concrete workflow rather than abstraction alone.
