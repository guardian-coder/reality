# Validation Participant Recruitment Shortlist

- **Date:** 2026-09-01
- **Status:** First outreach wave sent on 2026-09-01; see [OUTREACH_LOG.md](OUTREACH_LOG.md)
- **Geographic starting point:** Tanzania for validation access only; not a permanent market decision

## Recruitment target

Recruit a minimum validation cell:

1. one inventory-based SME with 6–12 months of records;
2. one SME lender or experienced credit analyst;
3. one qualified accountant familiar with SME bookkeeping and inventory.

The SME must not send sensitive records during initial outreach. Data collection begins only after scope, consent, de-identification, storage, access, and deletion procedures are agreed.

- Primary role: Manager, Youth and Enterprises Development
- Public contact: Castus Paschal, `castus.paschal@tncc.or.tz`, +255 778 879 537

**Request:** introduction to 3–5 established inventory-based SMEs—retail, wholesale, pharmacy, agricultural inputs, or FMCG distribution—with reasonably complete bank/mobile-money, sales, purchase, and inventory records.

### 2. Lender access — Kuunda

**Why first:** Kuunda publicly describes working-capital and embedded-liquidity products for agents and MSMEs, transaction-based decisioning, and Tanzanian operations. It is directly adjacent to the hypothesis being tested.

- Public route: founder-call/contact form on the company website
- Website: [Kuunda](https://www.kuunda.com/)
- Relevant context: Kuunda describes `Kazi Cash` for entrepreneurs and small businesses and partnerships with M-Pesa and FINCA.

**Request:** one product, risk, or credit professional for a 30-minute research interview about what transaction models establish, what still requires diligence, and what evidence would change an underwriting decision.

## Screening rules

- Do not recruit a participant solely because they are enthusiastic; require relevant records or professional experience.
- Do not promise financing, business benefit, confidentiality guarantees beyond the actual protocol, or compensation that has not been approved.
- Do not collect customer-level lender data or proprietary scoring logic.
- Do not receive raw SME records by ordinary email or messaging before a data protocol exists.
- Record refusals and reasons without collecting unnecessary personal data.

## Current uncertainty

Public contact information does not establish willingness, suitability, independence, or availability. The first three routes have been contacted, but nobody is a recruited participant until they explicitly agree and pass screening.
