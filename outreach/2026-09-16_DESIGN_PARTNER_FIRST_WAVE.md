## Core message

**Subject:** A question about how you verify an agent actually completed the job

Hi [Name],

I’m Brayan Lucas, building an open project called Reality.

I came across your work on [specific workflow]. You already measure real agent activity, so I’m hoping you can challenge a question we are testing:

When an agent spends money and takes an action, can the operator connect that cost to evidence from the system where the outcome actually appeared—not only to the agent’s own trace or a closed ticket?

We have built a small control-plane prototype that authorizes a paid action before execution, records its cost, and only counts the result after linked outcome evidence passes an explicit check. We do not yet know whether this solves a problem teams will pay for, or whether existing agent platforms already cover it well.

Could I ask you about one historical, non-sensitive workflow for 20 minutes? I am not asking for production access or a sales commitment. If the problem is already solved, that answer would be genuinely useful.

## Follow-up request if a gap appears

## What not to say

- Do not call the recipient a customer or design partner before they agree.
- Do not say Reality independently verifies evidence; source authenticity is still open.
- Do not claim existing observability tools cannot do this.
- Do not offer live control of production actions.
- Do not promise savings, safety, or ROI.
- Do not attach a long proposal to the first message.
