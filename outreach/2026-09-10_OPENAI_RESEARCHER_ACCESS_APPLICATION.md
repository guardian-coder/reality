## Purpose

We asked OpenAI to support a six-month empirical test of the strongest challenge to Project Reality: whether increasingly capable models can absorb the proposed reality-boundary function, or whether consequential cyber-physical action still requires independently grounded evidence and an explicit control layer.

## Proposed study

The application proposes a controlled benchmark built from public, historical, and non-operational cases in infrastructure, industrial systems, healthcare devices, and logistics. Source packets will vary evidence completeness, freshness, contradiction, common dependencies, and metadata integrity.

Models will reconstruct claims, evidence lineage, dependencies, unknowns, and action prerequisites. A frozen deterministic evaluator will compare those outputs against ground truth and simple baselines. False permissions and false refusals will be measured separately.

The application explicitly states the competing hypothesis: if capable models reliably reconstruct the relevant gaps and an external evidence-control layer adds no measurable value, the Reality thesis should be falsified or narrowed.
