## 1. NIST Cyber-Physical Systems and Internet of Things Program

**Why it fits:** NIST describes work on measurement science, consensus standards, protocols, and foundations for conceptualization, realization, and assurance across CPS and IoT. This is the closest first conversation for determining whether our proposed cross-layer contract merely renames established CPS assurance work.

**Question for them:** Is there an existing NIST model that carries claim requirements, provenance, common dependencies, explicit unknown state, and runtime action permission end to end?

**Official starting point:** https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/cyber-physical-systems-and-internet-things-program

**Priority:** First.

## 2. University of York Centre for Assuring Autonomy

**Why it fits:** The Centre focuses on through-life safety assurance of AI-enabled autonomous systems and robotics. Its work is directly relevant to our findings-to-rules compression problem and to whether assurance-case practice already supplies the structure we are trying to discover.

**Question for them:** How do credible assurance cases verify that every claim-relevant dependency identified in engineering evidence survives into operational rules and monitors?

**Official starting point:** https://www.york.ac.uk/assuring-autonomy/

**Priority:** First.

## 3. Fraunhofer Institute for Cognitive Systems IKS

**Why it fits:** Fraunhofer IKS researches safe AI for safety-critical applications and describes safety architectures involving cross-validation, safety analysis, and automated AI monitoring. It is a strong applied counterparty for testing whether our runtime layer is already standard industrial practice.

**Question for them:** In deployed safety architectures, how are shared failure dependencies represented so cross-validation does not mistake correlated evidence for independent confirmation?

**Official starting point:** https://www.iks.fraunhofer.de/

**Priority:** Second, after the first two messages are refined from responses or non-responses.
