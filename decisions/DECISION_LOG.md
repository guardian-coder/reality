# Decision Log

## D-001 — Seek infrastructure, not another generic AI application

- **Status:** Decided
- **Decision:** Search for a scarce layer that becomes more valuable as AI intelligence becomes abundant.
- **Rationale:** Intelligence itself and raw data alone are unlikely to be sufficient durable scarcity theses.

## D-002 — Use the AI-to-reality chain as the umbrella vision

- **Status:** Decided
- **Decision:** Explore infrastructure that lets intelligent systems interact with reality reliably.
- **Rationale:** Intent alone does not provide authority, execution, evidence, outcomes, or accountability.

## D-003 — Do not optimize thesis importance around solo execution

- **Status:** Decided
- **Decision:** Do not rank opportunities primarily by current skills, capital, location, or single-founder buildability.
- **Rationale:** Importance and entry strategy are separate questions.

## D-004 — Do not select trading

- **Status:** Decided
- **Decision:** Trading is not the company opportunity under exploration.

## D-005 — Do not copy Scale AI mechanically

- **Status:** Decided
- **Decision:** Use the pattern technological wave → hidden bottleneck → scarce asset → infrastructure; do not copy a particular founder or company.

- **Not decided:** Product, customer, payer, market, geography, or final company scope.

## D-009 — Clarify the candidate primitive and the role of SMEs

- **Status:** Decided as the current conceptual definition; empirical validity remains unproven
- **Decision:** Define the candidate primitive as a repeatable, measurable evidence-to-state process that produces a defensible claim sufficient for a specified consequential decision.
- **Decision:** Treat SME financial truth as the first laboratory, not as the company identity or assumed final market.
- **Rationale:** The same abstract pattern may underlie SME underwriting, military actionable intelligence, health, and other domains: many signals must become a supported state claim before action. The process must be inspectable and not depend solely on one expert's intuition.
- **Uncertainty:** Cross-domain portability is a hypothesis, not a demonstrated capability or market decision.

## D-010 — Separate extraction, estimation, and verification

- **Status:** Decided as analytical framework; scarcity thesis remains unproven
- **Decision:** Treat information extraction, state estimation, and decision-grade verification as distinct capabilities.
- **Decision:** Do not claim information extraction alone as the primitive; test whether scarcity persists in state estimation and especially defensible verification as AI improves.
- **Decision:** Call “defensible reality for consequential decisions” a candidate invariant until it survives explicit counterexamples, willingness-to-pay tests, incumbent comparisons, and repeatability tests.
- **Rationale:** A coherent, recurring idea is not evidence that the market needs it or that advancing models will not commoditize it.
