# Principles

1. **Importance before ease.** Do not shrink the opportunity thesis around solo execution, present skills, present capital, or current location.
2. **Falsify before building.** Attack the assumption most capable of collapsing the thesis before substantial product code.
3. **Evidence over narrative.** Separate sourced evidence, inference, hypothesis, decision, and unknown.
4. **Reality over raw data.** Data without economic or operational meaning is not automatically truth.
5. **Decision relevance matters.** Better information is valuable only if it changes a consequential decision or action.
6. **Expose uncertainty.** A defensible system shows confidence, contradictions, provenance, and unknowns.
7. **Do not confuse wedge with vision.** A practical entry point must not silently redefine the company-level problem.
8. **Do not copy outcomes.** Learn the pattern technological wave → hidden bottleneck → scarce asset → infrastructure; do not imitate a prior company mechanically.
9. **The repository is durable memory.** Important evidence and decisions must not remain buried in chat.
10. **Change state explicitly.** Update the decision log and AI context whenever the project's authoritative position changes.
