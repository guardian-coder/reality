# Founding Memo

## The observation

AI is making cognition, prediction, planning, and software action cheaper. Intelligence can form an intent, but intelligence alone cannot reliably cause an accountable change in the world.

The working chain is:

> Intelligence → decision / intent → authority → action → real-world state change → evidence → outcome → accountability / learning

We are searching for the scarce infrastructure underneath this chain: a layer that becomes more valuable as AI becomes more capable, rather than being rapidly commoditized by that capability.

## The company-level thesis

The umbrella vision is to build infrastructure that lets intelligent systems interact with reality reliably.

“Reliably” may require trustworthy context, permission, verification, execution evidence, contradiction handling, and accountability. Which of these is the deepest primitive remains unresolved.

## The current wedge

The working wedge is **Decision-Grade Financial Truth for SMEs**.

Economic activity increasingly leaves fragmented digital traces: bank and mobile-money transactions, invoices, supplier payments, inventory movements, payroll, tax records, and other evidence. A transaction proves that money moved; it does not by itself prove the economic meaning of that movement.

The hypothesized valuable object is:

> Verified economic context + evidence → a defensible representation of business reality

That representation should expose its evidence, contradictions, confidence, and unresolved uncertainty so it can support a consequential decision.

## Why this is only a wedge

Financial truth is treated as one testable entry into the larger ground-truth / AI-to-reality problem. It is not a decision to permanently limit the company to finance, SMEs, or any geography.

We explicitly do not rank the importance of a thesis by whether one founder can build it alone, by present capital, by current skills, or by local accessibility. Entry strategy may consider these constraints later; the search for the important scarce layer must not.

## What must be proven next

Can existing digital traces plus modern AI already reconstruct SME economic truth well enough for consequential decisions?

- If yes, this wedge may collapse into an analytics feature owned by banks, accounting platforms, mobile-money operators, or credit bureaus.
- If no, and missing meaning requires new evidence, verification, reconciliation, or real-world context, the infrastructure thesis becomes stronger.

No product, customer, payer, business model, or initial geography has yet been validated.
