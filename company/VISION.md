# Vision

## Umbrella vision

Build infrastructure that lets intelligent systems interact with reality reliably.

## Long-range thesis

As intelligence becomes abundant and cheap, value may migrate toward inputs and capabilities intelligence cannot manufacture directly: trustworthy context, authority, institutional permission, physical capacity, verified outcomes, and accountability.

## The reliability problem

An intelligent system can propose a decision. A real-world outcome still depends on a chain of truth, authority, execution, evidence, and accountability. Failure anywhere in that chain can make the result unsafe, unverifiable, or economically useless.

## Current expression

Decision-Grade Truth means an evidence-backed claim about real-world state sufficiently reliable for a specified consequential decision. “Sufficient” depends on the decision, its error costs, and its evidence standard; it does not mean absolute or universal truth.

The first chosen industry is the Internet of Battlefield Things / connected battlefield systems. The focus is maintaining trustworthy correspondence between battlefield reality and the internal state used by intelligent systems despite missing observations, stale data, conflicting sources, deception, degraded communications, and environmental change.

SME economic reality remains useful prior falsification work, but it is no longer the primary industry direction. The cross-domain hypothesis remains unproven.

## Current mission boundary

Project Reality is investigating assurance infrastructure: provenance, identity, freshness, contradiction detection, uncertainty, state confidence, revalidation, and post-action outcome verification. It is not presently pursuing weapon targeting or autonomous attack capability.

## Unresolved architecture

We do not yet know whether Reality Coupling becomes a distinct product layer, a framework spanning existing IoBT components, or a feature captured by incumbent sensing, fusion, assurance, or command systems. The relationship between Decision-Grade Truth and Trusted Execution / Delegated Authority also remains unresolved.
